Conversations by the Sea

Context

This is a narrative excerpt from an unfinished story I was working on around 2022. I really liked this specific interaction between these two characters; it felt real, these character emotions.

These two characters exist in a world that is at war, and in this moment they get to be at peace just for a second, contemplating the meaning of life. Enjoying their youth for the first time, feeling like the kids they are for the first time in a long time, before they were handed guns to their hands by their elders, who promised peace and prosperity for the next generations, only to be met with death and violence.

These two characters are dear friends that met at the academy before war started, since then they became inseparable. In this moment they share their thoughts on life, as well as allowing themselves to be vulnerable with their emotions, truthful to each other, thus deepening their friendship and understanding of one another.

Conversations by the Sea

“Do you think we have a purpose in life, Zumi?” Asahi asked.

The ocean breeze was fresh in a weird refreshing way considering it was fall. His eyes moved from the camp stationed up in the mountain to hers. She could see herself in his eyes. She could feel his pain, his happiness, his sadness and how lost he felt. She felt it all too. She wished she didn't. But they were so lost between what was wrong or right. All they ever yearned for was a stable happy life. But this wasn't it, life was unforgiving and punishing. If they wanted happiness they would have to fight for it, they would have to make sacrifices. The question was which ones were the right ones.

She could read him like an open book, and so did he. She never had a friend like this before. She never had a friend at all, besides her dead brother. She wished she could be talking with him in late afternoons like this one forever. Because when she was with him, she felt like the world wasn’t falling apart as much.

“I don't know…” She said as she looked at the ground fidgeting with her fingers.

“Some say it's love. I’ve never fallen in love. Don’t think I should. It looks so painful and I already have enough pain in me.”

“Well, I love you. Not in a partner way, in a friendship way, but stronger somehow… Like if we were brothers, bonded in a way.”

“I love you too Zumi.” He said as he chuckled, dismissing her need to justify everything. “But don’t you think that love might be a poisoned cake?”

“How so?”

“Looks lovely, enchanting, glamorous… But it can kill you from the inside out.”

She looked at him attentively trying to find a hint of something. Had he fallen in love before?

He seemed to understand her thought.

“Oh don't look at me like that. It's not from experience. It's my mom. She fell in love with my father but all he did was break her into pieces.”

Zumi agreed in silence, thinking back to her very own parents. And soon, she figured out, she had never seen true love in real life, only in stories. Was love something so special that only stories could have it?

“I don’t think life has a purpose." He said at last.

“So you think being alive is the same as being dead?”

“Yes. And sometimes you don't even know if you are really alive because you feel so dead inside.”

“Asahi-” She started. He cut her off.

“Oh I'm fine. Don't worry about me.”

She looked at him wondering if he was just saying that so she felt better or if he was trying to convince himself.

“I’m fine when I’m with you." He started, gazing upon at the vast sea. "You make me feel alive.” he concluded.
