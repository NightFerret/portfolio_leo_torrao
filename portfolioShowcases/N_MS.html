My Station

Context

"My Station" is a university project conducted in my second year on the Undergraduate of Games and Multimedia. This was made for our photography class, where we could choose any theme, and make a photography project about it. I chose to make a photography project about nostalgia and loss, using a train station as the subject of the passage of time.

My Station

The station has seen me grow, these trains have carried me my whole life. I now look into them and remember my childish wonder on their complex mechanics. My innocent admiration of their power and greatness. I look into them and I see a part of myself lost somewhere. A train that I missed, a train that I lost, a train that I try to find in every other train, but no train has it. In no train I get back my colour.

Now the world is a different shade. Now the colours are not what they used to be. Now the station is empty, now the tracks are dirty. Now everything is lost to nostalgia, and the lack of capability of bringing a moment back is tiring.

Now I can’t be in the same station as them. They are in the night and I’m stuck in the day. I watch as they part away and as I stay. I watch as colour slowly fades away from me. I watch as my station gets empty. There is still hope, there is still day, but the colour we lost is forever lost, and no train can bring it back. So I wait, and wait, and wait... I wait to see if a train will bring them back into my station.
