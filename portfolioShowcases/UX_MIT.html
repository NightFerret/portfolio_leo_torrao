Swap & Sound

Usability Research

Context

This project goal was for me and my team to conduct usability tests for another team and present results. Planning and moderation of playtests focused on usability, using semi structured interviews. The selected team we worked with were the creators of the game swap and sound. Besides the usability tests and analysis of these, we also had to name some possible applicable accessibility features for their game.

What have I learned with this project?

This university project was really insightful on how to properly conduct Usability tests on video games, as well as how to present the collected data.

I really liked working on this project because it was fun and because I had never done any playtest with this much rigor in the steps and in the documentation to be presented. In the end we did a presentation to the team we were working in to present the gathered information, which was a really nice representation of how it would actually unfold.

Swap & Sound

Team Name: Pure Honey Studios

Game Name: Swap & Sound

Genre: Puzzle & Resource Management

Main Inspirations: The Forest Quartet (2022, Bedtime Digital Games); A Short Hike (2019, Adam Robinson-Yu); Donut County (2018, Annapurna Interactive).

Description:

An alien ship was circling Earth's orbit when it suddenly had to make an emergency stop. It crashed into a ceiling, causing a strange quake that sent waves through the house. Now, the homeowner must clean up the mess by putting all the sounds back where they belong.

Swap & Sound is a puzzle / resource management game that follows a human who had an alien ship crash into his ceiling. You, as the human, are now tasked with solving the mess created by the crash, a sound mess.

Game Mechanics & Systems

In this section I’ll talk about the game mechanics and game systems present in Swap & sound, so that later on we can perform an accurate Usability Expert Analysis.

Retrieving & Placing (puzzle solving)

This mechanic corresponds to the main mechanic. Using the alien tool, we will be able to retrieve and place sounds; the puzzles will be made around this.

Slime Rancher (2017, Monomi Park)

Slime Rancher is a game where you use a gun to pull items and collect them

The Forest Quartet (2022, Mads & Friends)

In The Forest Quartet you play as a girl and you have to pick up sounds to place them in their correct place

Portal 2 (2011, Valve Corporation)

Portal 2 is a game where you use a gun to grab and drop boxes to clear puzzles.

Identifying Errors (puzzle solving)

Part of the Puzzle system is not only retrieving and placing, but also identifying errors

The Forest Quartet (2022, Mads & Friends)

In The Forest Quartet you have to be able to identify if the sound you have picked up fits where you are putting it in

Papers Please (2013, Lucas Pope)

In Papers Please you have to process the information given and identify if there is forged information.

Please Fix The Road (2022, ARIELEK)

In Please Fix The Road a road layout is given, and the player has to be able to identify what is wrong with the layout and fix it.

Managing Resources

The game also has a system to allow the player to store sounds. Of course, by having this storage they will have to manage it

Slime Rancher (2017, Monomi Park)

In Slime Rancher you have to collect items and you have to manage them to see if you use them or save them for later

Please Fix The Road (2022, ARIELEK)

In Please Fix The Road the layout is strictly limited by the pieces given for the puzzle, you can't use "more road" than what is given in the beginning of the puzzle.

Portal 2 (2011, Valve Corporation)

In Portal 2 the player can't use more than two portals at once, the player has to manage which portals should stay up.
